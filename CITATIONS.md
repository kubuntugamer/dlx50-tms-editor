## Verification methodology

- **Cases:** each Justia URL was fetched during drafting; the page title and
  reported citation in the URL were confirmed to match the case cited in the
  brief (e.g. fetching `/F2/977/1510/305345/` returned *"Sega Enterprises,
  Ltd. v. Accolade, Inc., 977 F.2d 1510 (9th Cir. 1992)"*).
- **Statutes:** the Cornell pattern `/uscode/text/<title>/<section>/` was
  verified live against 17 U.S.C. §102, whose retrieved text shows the
  §102(b) exclusion quoted in the brief.
- **Official sources:** the Copyright Office fair-use pages and the Uniform
  Law Commission's UTSA PDF were fetched directly; the EU link is the
  official EUR-Lex ELI identifier for the directive.
- **Known gap:** the *Lotus* Supreme Court affirmance (516 U.S. 696) has no
  free standalone opinion page — it is a one-page affirmance by an equally
  divided Court; the First Circuit opinion below is the substantive decision.
